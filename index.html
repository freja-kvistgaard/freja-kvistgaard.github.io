About me

I am a PhD student at the Department of Economics and Business Economics at Aarhus University and affiliated with TrygFonden’s Centre for Child Research.

My research is in the economics of education. I am particularly interested in teachers, teacher value added, and how students’ educational experiences shape their outcomes.
